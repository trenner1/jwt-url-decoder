# Privacy policy

JWT URL Decoder does not collect, store, or transmit any data.

- The extension reads the URL of your browser tabs to detect JSON Web Tokens and decodes them on your device.
- It makes no network requests. Nothing you view with it is sent to the developer or to any third party.
- It stores nothing. Decoded tokens exist only while the side panel is showing them.
- It does not read or modify the content of the pages you visit.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/trenner1/jwt-url-decoder/issues.
